Split Pay — three style options

Same flow in all three: four bottom tabs, group list, expense list, Add expense button. Only the look changes. All names and numbers are sample data.

A — Ink & Lime

Dark, bold, modern. Big numbers, one bright accent, floating menu bar. Easy on the eyes at night.

B — Paper Ledger

Light, calm, like a tidy notebook. Thin lines instead of boxes, classic number font, full-width Add button.

C — Soft Pop

Light, friendly, colourful. Rounded cards, each group gets its own colour, playful for a group of friends.
